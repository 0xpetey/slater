# Two OCR readings, not Vision's confidence score, decide Low-confidence Blocks

Measured on the test fixtures, Vision's confidence score doesn't reflect accuracy. It gave 1.00 to a Line with a dropped character (取り消す read as 取りす) and 0.30–0.50 to correctly read short words like 品番. Preprocessing is also a double-edged tool: upscaling and contrast rescue small text and blurry scans, but make Vision drop characters from clean text (担当者 read as 担者). So Slater reads every crop twice, raw and preprocessed, in parallel. Where the readings agree the text is trusted. Where they differ, the longer reading wins, because dropped characters are the usual failure, and the Block is marked Low-confidence. This roughly doubles OCR work, and that's the price of catching misreads that would otherwise be silent.
